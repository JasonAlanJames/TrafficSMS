Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information created through your use of TrafficSMS, and limited technical information needed to secure and improve the service.

Identity and contact data

This includes your name, email address, phone number, and billing address when you create an account or subscribe.

Technical and device data

We may collect IP address, browser type, device information, login history, and related diagnostic data to operate the platform safely.

Service activity data

We store traffic requests, saved routes, subscription information, and account preferences so we can deliver personalized traffic updates.

Security and support data

When you contact support or interact with security systems, we may create logs and internal notes necessary to protect the service and respond to issues.

2

Account Registration

You can create a TrafficSMS account using an email and password or by choosing Google Sign In when available. During registration, we use the information you provide to establish your profile, enable account access, and connect your subscription and message preferences.

TrafficSMS uses Firebase Authentication to support account creation, identity verification, and secure sign-in flows. We only request information that is reasonably necessary to provide the service, manage your subscription, and communicate with you about your account.

3

Google/Firebase Authentication

If you choose Google Sign In, Google provides authentication information that helps us verify your identity without requiring a separate password. Firebase Authentication manages authentication workflows, issues security tokens, and maintains authentication sessions used to keep you signed in securely.

  • Google Sign In can provide basic account identity details such as your name and email address.
  • Firebase Authentication helps us validate credentials, rotate security tokens, and manage active sessions.
  • We use session and token data to prevent unauthorized access and to protect account integrity across devices.
4

Stripe Payment Processing

TrafficSMS never stores your full payment card details on our own systems. Stripe securely processes subscription payments, invoices, billing events, and saved payment methods on our behalf.

We may store limited billing metadata such as subscription status, invoice references, customer identifiers, and payment state so we can manage your service. For more information about how Stripe handles personal data, review the Stripe Privacy Policy.

5

Twilio SMS Messaging

TrafficSMS uses Twilio to send and receive SMS messages. This includes incoming SMS commands, outgoing traffic responses, message logs, delivery status updates, and subscription-control keywords such as STOP, HELP, and START.

  • Opt-In and messaging setupWe process your phone number and consent status to confirm opt-in before sending recurring subscription messages.
  • Opt-Out handlingIf you send STOP, we record that preference and stop non-essential SMS traffic to that number unless you later send START or otherwise re-subscribe.
  • Support keywordsHELP requests may trigger automated guidance or support information so you can understand how to use the service.
  • Carrier considerationsStandard carrier messaging and data fees may apply depending on your wireless provider and plan.
6

Traffic Request History

To help you revisit useful information and improve service continuity, we may store traffic searches, recent lookups, traffic summaries, and favorite locations linked to your account. This history supports features such as faster repeat searches, personalized routing context, and continuity between SMS and web experiences.

7

Saved Routes

TrafficSMS allows you to store route-related information such as Home, Work, custom routes, and alerts. We use this information to help generate faster traffic responses, maintain route preferences, and deliver relevant updates when those features are enabled.

You control what route information you save, update, or remove from your account. Removing a route may affect related personalization and alert functionality.

8

Location Information

TrafficSMS may use approximate location data, ZIP Code, city, state, or manual location entry to return relevant traffic information. We may also use Google Maps geocoding services to translate addresses, place names, or route descriptions into map coordinates and traffic queries.

TrafficSMS may support GPS-based features in the future. If introduced, those features will be governed by the permissions you grant on your device and the disclosures shown in the product at that time.
9

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep TrafficSMS secure and usable.

  • Authentication cookies help recognize signed-in users.
  • Session cookies support secure navigation and temporary state while you use the service.
  • Security cookies help detect abuse, protect sign-in flows, and reduce fraud risk.
  • Preference cookies remember settings such as saved choices and interface preferences.
10

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics and related measurement tools to understand site performance, monitor feature usage, and evaluate anonymous usage statistics. Analytics data helps us improve speed, reliability, conversion flows, and the overall user experience.

Where practical, we rely on aggregated or de-identified information to understand trends rather than to identify individual users directly.

11

Artificial Intelligence

TrafficSMS may use Amazon Bedrock to support natural language processing, traffic summaries, prompt processing, and question answering features. These systems help us interpret user requests and return useful traffic-related responses through the web app or SMS workflows.

TrafficSMS does not use customer data to train foundation models unless we explicitly disclose that practice and provide any required notice or choice.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide TrafficSMS, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the service.

  • AccountsAccount information may be retained while your profile remains active and for a reasonable period after closure for legal, security, and operational purposes.
  • SubscriptionsBilling and subscription records may be retained as needed for financial reporting, fraud prevention, charge management, and tax compliance.
  • Traffic history and logsTraffic history, diagnostic logs, and related service records may be retained according to operational needs, security review cycles, and backup practices.
  • Deleted accountsWhen you delete an account, we will work to remove or de-identify information, subject to retention needs for legal compliance, abuse prevention, and legitimate business records.
14

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), subject to applicable exceptions.

  • Right to Know what categories of personal information we collect, use, disclose, and retain.
  • Right to Access certain personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Delete personal information, subject to lawful exceptions.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in the Contact Information section below. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

15

Children's Privacy

TrafficSMS is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to TrafficSMS, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

16

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Encryption and HTTPS

We use encrypted connections, including HTTPS, to protect data in transit where supported.

Password hashing

Passwords and credentials are protected using industry-standard authentication and hashing practices through our identity providers.

Access controls

Internal access is limited to authorized personnel and service systems with a legitimate operational need.

Infrastructure security and monitoring

We maintain logging, monitoring, and provider-level security controls to detect issues and respond to suspicious activity.

Your Choices

You may have several choices regarding how you use TrafficSMS and how we communicate with you.

  • Update profile information and saved preferences through your account when those features are available.
  • Request account deletion or data export by contacting support.
  • Send STOP to opt out of SMS marketing or subscription text messages, subject to service design and legal requirements.
  • Send HELP for service guidance and support information.
  • Unsubscribe from promotional communications using the instructions included in those messages where applicable.
